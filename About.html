Our Story

Passionate about coffee, dedicated to community. Discover the people and values behind every cup.

How It All Began

The Espresso was founded in 2026 by a group of friends who love coffee. The shop started as a small café in the middle of Belfast and quickly turned into a small community.

We source our beans from sustainable farms in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala as this builds a lasting relationship with growers who care as much about quality as we do.

Small details make a big difference in coffee like the water temperature and the way it's poured. We put a lot of time and effort into how our coffee is made.
